Here's the Scenario

You just got your first full-time job and you're living on your own. You need to figure out how to spend your money on everything: housing, food, transportation, insurance, clothes, and more.

20 beans = your income

Each bean represents a portion of your paycheck. You'll split them across 11 budget categories, but you won't have enough for everything you want.

What to Expect

  1. Round 1: Full Income Set up your budget with 20 beans. Choose what matters to you.
  2. Round 2: Income Cut Your hours get cut. Now you only have 13 beans. What do you sacrifice?
  3. Round 3: Life Happens Random events test your choices. Did you plan well enough?

There's no right answer. Every choice is a trade-off. Spending more on one thing means less for something else. Pay attention to what you prioritize and what you're willing to give up.
