Compound Growth Time Machine

Compound growth is the money your money earns just by sitting there. What you earn gets added to your balance, then that larger balance earns too, and the cycle keeps going.

Money grows in two different ways, and they are not the same. A savings account pays interest at a rate the bank sets. Investments earn returns, from changes in price plus any dividends. This activity is about investing, so everything ahead is a rate of return.

How it works

  1. First, a quick demo showing how what you earn starts earning too.
  2. Then you'll make four predictions about how money grows in different scenarios.
  3. After each guess, we'll show you what actually happens and ask you to reflect on why.
  4. At the end, you'll build your own time machine and lock in your key takeaways.
