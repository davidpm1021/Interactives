Compound Interest Time Machine

Compound interest is the money your money earns just by sitting in an account that pays interest, like a savings account or an investment account. Your original deposit earns interest, then that interest earns interest right alongside your deposit, and the cycle keeps going.

How it works

  1. First, a quick demo showing how interest earns interest.
  2. Then you'll make four predictions about how money grows in different scenarios.
  3. After each guess, we'll show you what actually happens and ask you to reflect on why.
  4. At the end, you'll build your own time machine and lock in your key takeaways.
