Assumptions

  • Expected return: {{ (inputs().expectedReturn * 100).toFixed(1) }}% real return per year, compounded monthly.
  • Withdrawal rate: 4% of the nest egg per year (a rough industry rule of thumb).
  • Retirement horizon: {{ inputs().yearsInRetirement }} years, ending around age {{ inputs().retirementAge + inputs().yearsInRetirement }}.
  • What's not modeled: Social Security, employer match, taxes, inflation on your target budget, market volatility.
