Retirement Calculator

Try different scenarios. See the gap between what your savings will grow to and what you'd need to fund your retirement budget.

Assumptions
  • Life expectancy: 95 years old.
  • Pre-retirement return: 6% per year.
  • Post-retirement return: 5% per year on the balance you haven't withdrawn yet.
  • Inflation: 3% per year. Your target budget is inflated to retirement year, and the yearly withdrawal grows 3% each year of retirement.
  • Salary growth: 2% per year. Your monthly contribution grows 2% each year too (treating it as a constant share of income).
  • What's not modeled: Social Security, employer match, taxes, market volatility.
