Nice work.

You've walked through writing a check, written two on your own, and learned to spot the most common problems. Those are the building blocks of check literacy.

Key takeaways

  • Numeric and written amounts must match. Double-check both before signing.
  • Always include the cents fraction. "and XX/100" closes the door on someone adding cents after you sign.
  • No signature, no check. Without one, the bank has no authorization to release the money.
  • The memo line is optional, but useful. It gives you a record of what each check was for.
