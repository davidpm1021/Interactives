Salary is not net worth

Two real-looking people. Marcus earns twice what Priya does. Look at what happens when you add up everything each of them actually owns and owes.

Three numbers, three different meanings

Salary

A rate. How fast money comes in over a year.

Cash on hand

A snapshot of the money you can spend right now.

Net worth

A balance. Everything you own minus everything you owe. This is the one that tells you where you actually stand.

Marcus's paychecks are twice as big, but he stands under water because his debts add up to more than his single asset. Priya's smaller salary bought her a house, and that house is why she's above the line.
