Salary, cash, and net worth are three different things.

  • Salary is a rate. How fast money comes in each year.
  • Cash on hand is a snapshot. What sits in your accounts today.
  • Net worth is the balance. Everything you own minus everything you owe.

A big salary can still leave you with negative net worth if debts stack up faster than assets grow. A modest salary can build real wealth when you own things that appreciate.
